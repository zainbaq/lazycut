# Playbook for the Phase 0 test: how the boss_fight event types appear in this game.
# Written by Claude from web research on 2026-11-14 (not by the app yet).
# Only what the sources support is stated; gaps are listed under `unknowns`.

topic: "Phantom Blade Zero"
coverage: partial
summary: >
  Fast wuxia sword combat. Dangerous enemy attacks are colour-coded by a flash on
  the enemy just before they land: a blue flash (Brutal Move) can be parried, a
  red flash (Killer Move) cannot and must be dodged. A perfectly timed parry of a
  blue attack, or a perfectly timed dodge of a red one, triggers a Ghoststep: the
  player character abruptly relocates behind the enemy and gets a free attack on
  its back. Both fighters have a Sha-Chi gauge next to health. Blocking drains the
  player's Sha-Chi, not health. Emptying the enemy's Sha-Chi breaks its guard and
  opens it to an Execution.

entries:
  - event_type: counter
    known_as: "Ghoststep (also written Ghost Step or Spectral Step)"
    looks_like: >
      The enemy flashes blue or red, then at the moment of impact the player
      character abruptly relocates behind the enemy and strikes its back.
      Report the moment of the relocation.
    not_to_confuse_with: >
      An ordinary block or deflection where the player stays in front of the
      enemy (that is a parry), or a plain dodge with no relocation behind the enemy.

  - event_type: parry
    known_as: "block or parry without a Ghoststep"
    looks_like: >
      The player's weapon meets the enemy's attack and the player holds position
      in front of the enemy. No relocation behind it.
    not_to_confuse_with: >
      A hit taken. A blocked attack drains the player's Sha-Chi gauge but leaves
      the health bar unchanged.

  - event_type: hit_taken
    known_as: "health loss"
    looks_like: >
      The player's health bar itself gets shorter. Find the health bar and the
      Sha-Chi gauge in the first seconds and tell them apart before counting.
    not_to_confuse_with: >
      Blocked attacks, the Sha-Chi gauge dropping, the player being pushed back
      while guarding, or the player character drinking or using an item. None of
      these are hits unless the health bar gets shorter.

  - event_type: boss_attack
    known_as: "Brutal Move (blue flash) or Killer Move (red flash)"
    looks_like: >
      A flash of blue or red on the enemy just before a heavy attack. Say which
      colour in the label.

  - event_type: phase_change
    looks_like: >
      Bosses in this game often have two or three phases. Look for a short
      cinematic, a change in the boss's weapon or stance, or its health bar refilling.

  - event_type: finisher
    known_as: "Execution"
    looks_like: >
      After the boss's guard is broken, a button prompt appears above it, followed
      by a short cinematic killing animation. Report the first frame of that
      animation for the kill that ends the fight.
    not_to_confuse_with: >
      Executions or guard breaks earlier in the fight that do not end it.

screen_notes: >

  Work it out from the first seconds of the footage and say so in BASICS.
